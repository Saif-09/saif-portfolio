## Problem

Billi ke liye koi chidiya ya machhli ka video lagao aur maza kareeb das second
chalta hai: keyboard par pehla panja playback pause kar deta hai, video skip kar
deta hai, ya kuch random khol deta hai. Billi jeet jaati hai, video ruk jaata hai,
aur aap ise phir theek karne uthte hain. Cat Mode wo chhota fix hai: billi ko
screen par jhapatne do, bina uske panje browser ko control kiye.

## Decisions

**Ek click, sab kuch freeze.** Poora product ek toggle hai. Ise dabao aur Cat Mode
keyboard, mouse, trackpad, scroll, aur touch freeze kar deta hai, taaki video har
jhapatte, headbutt, aur desk par poore sharir ki phislan ke beech chalta rahe.
Billi ke beech mein settings mein uljhne ki zaroorat nahi.

**Bahar nikalne ka sirf-insaan wala rasta.** Saara input block karne se ek saaf
sawaal uthta hai: ise wapas kaise band karein. Unlock ek keyboard shortcut hai
(default Ctrl+Shift+K, customizable), isliye chuna kyunki ye waisa socha-samjha
key-combination hai jise ek billi asal mein nahi daba sakti par ek insaan ek second
mein daba deta hai.

**Local, private, koi account nahi.** Na kuch sign-in karne ko hai, na kuch track
hota hai. Sab kuch browser mein locally chalta hai. Itna chhota toy aapka data nahi
chahna chahiye, to nahi chahta.

**Tone ko use case se match karo.** Poori cheez soch-samajhkar bewkoofana hai:
pounce-proof, billi emoji, chanchal copy, kyunki ek cat-video locker ko serious
software dikhana galat hota. Sanyam yahi hai ki ek kaam achhe se karo aur use sajao
mat.
