## Problem

Main chahta tha ki mera Mac lid band rakhte hue bhi chalta rahe. Dim nahi: asal
mein kaam karta hua, raatbhar ek AI agent, ek Xcode archive, ek lamba sync, lid
band, koi external monitor nahi. macOS ise clamshell sleep kehta hai, aur use
parwah nahi ki aapka kaam beech mein hai.

Pech mechanism mein hai. Popular keep-awake tools IOKit power assertions use karte
hain, aur wo clamshell sleep ko tab tak nahi rokte jab tak aapke paas ek external
display, power, aur ek keyboard na juda ho, thik wahi setup jisse main bachna
chahta tha. Iklauta lever jo bina kuch jode kaam karta hai, wo ek privileged
command hai: pmset disablesleep. To poora product asal mein ek hi cheez hai, ek
khatarnak system setting ke ird-gird ek safe wrapper, aur baaki sab kuch us ek
lever ko safe aur sukhad tarike se kheenchne layak banane ke liye hai.

## Decisions

**Safety feature hai, jage rehna nahi.** Ek generic paid keep-awake app shuru mein
hi mara hua hai: Amphetamine aur caffeinate pehle se muft mein ye karte hain. Bina
serve kiya gaya angle tha garmi. Band lid airflow ko ghonta hai, aur koi maujooda
tool iske baare mein ek shabd nahi kehta. To Insomniac "jage raho" nahi hai, ye
"machine ko garam kiye bina jage raho" hai.

**macOS jo imaandari se expose karta hai use maapo, nakli degrees nahi.** Lubhawna
version ek CPU temperature padhta hai aur degrees mein ek countdown dikhata hai.
Par Apple Silicon die temperature padhne ka koi supported tarika expose nahi
karta; jo kuch bhi aisa dawa karta hai wo private keys padh raha hai jo models ke
beech tootti hain. To maine ProcessInfo.thermalState (nominal, fair, serious,
critical) par banaya, jise koi admin nahi chahiye aur jo hamesha ke liye supported
hai. Aur asli suraksha ek upfront prediction hai hi nahi: ye ek live thermal
cutoff hai jo lid band rehte hue state par nazar rakhti hai aur agar ye badhe to
session auto-stop kar deti hai. Ek prediction galat ho sakta hai; ek live cutoff
ground truth hai.

**Ek architecture jahan khatarnak hissa swappable hai.** pmset ko root ki tarah
chalane ke do raste hain: ek AppleScript prompt jo har toggle par password maangta
hai (maamuli, pehle din kaam karta hai) aur XPC par ek privileged helper jo chupke
se toggle karta hai (production-grade, signing aur ek pinned contract ki zaroorat).
App ko kisi ek se jodne ke bajaye, maine dono ko ek PowerControlling protocol ke
peeche chhipaya. Maine pehle din password-prompt version ship kiya aur baad mein
silent helper bina kisi caller badlaav ke joda. Helper locked down hai: ek root
daemon ki tarah ye sirf un XPC connections ko accept karta hai jo is app ke
identifier aur Team ID se pinned hon.

**Safety invariants code dwara laagu kiye jaate hain.** Ek keep-awake app mein ek
vinaashkari failure hai: kaam khatam hone ke baad sleep ko disabled chhod dena.
Maine "sleep ko kabhi disabled na chhodo" ko ek invariant maana aur har exit band
kiya. Toggle off, auto-off, thermal cutoff, aur battery cutoff sab sleep restore
karte hain. Quit aur logout termination ko tab tak taalte hain jab tak restore
poora na ho; app sleep disabled rehte hue marne se inkaar karta hai. Ek hard crash
intercept nahi kiya ja sakta, to agle launch par app bina kisi active session ke
sleep disabled paata hai aur ise reset karne ki peshkash karta hai. Koi
"indefinite" bhi nahi hai: sabse lamba session ek sakht 8-ghante ki seema hai,
kyunki "forever" thik wahi setting hai jo ek mara hua, garam laptop bag mein
chhod deti hai.

**Imaandar UI, imaandar privacy.** App sirf menu-bar hai. Advisory ek risk level
(low, moderate, high) aur ek saada message dikhati hai, kabhi wo degrees nahi jinhe
main imaandari se nahi padh sakta, aur weather nudge soch-samajhkar sabse kamzor
input hai: ek garam kamra risk badha sakta hai, par ek thanda kamra ek garam hote
CPU ko kabhi safe nahi bana sakta. Weather location IP geolocation se aati hai,
city-level, to koi GPS permission prompt nahi; weather band karo aur app kuch nahi
bhejta. Koi telemetry nahi, koi analytics nahi.

**Wo bug jo mujhe sirf iske saath jeekar mila.** V1 ne Mac ko jagaye rakha, par
screen lid band rehte hue jalti rahi, kyunki sleep disable karna macOS ko internal
display band karne se bhi rokta hai. Keyboard ke khilaf jalta ek backlight barbaad
battery aur garmi hai, thik wahi cheez jisse app bachata hai. Maine ek lid monitor
joda jo open-se-closed edge par fire hota hai aur display ko ek harmless user-level
command se sleep karata hai, privileged helper se soch-samajhkar alag rakha gaya.
Jis cheez ko iski zaroorat nahi, uske liye least privilege.

**Abhi ship karo, rough edge ko apnao.** Core feature ko App Sandbox off chahiye,
jo Mac App Store ko bahar kar deta hai, to maine ek direct download ship kiya.
Pehle Apple ki paperwork par din bitane ke bajaye, maine abhi ek un-notarized build
ship kiya aur natijatan aane wali Gatekeeper warning ko ek guided, ek-minute ka
step banaya: installer ek haath se banayi setup guide hai, quarantine-clearing
command app ke thik bagal mein ek copy karne layak file ki tarah hai, aur app har
launch par apna quarantine flag hata deta hai taaki warning kabhi wapas na aaye.
Packaging script ek certificate maujood hote hi ek command se re-sign aur notarize
kar deti hai.

## Outcome

Insomniac [live](https://saif-09.github.io/insomniac/) hai: ek menu-bar app jo Mac
ko lid-band jagaye rakhta hai, ek live thermal cutoff, ek battery cutoff, crash
recovery, aur ek signed helper ke zariye silent toggling ke saath. Poori cheez do
niyamon par tiki hai: imaandar raho (asli mechanism, nakli degrees ke bajaye risk
levels, ek kamzor signal ko kamzor rakha) aur rough edges ko chhipane ke bajaye
apnate hue tezi se ship karo. Ek keep-awake app ek chhoti cheez hai, par ye ek aisi
setting ko chhuta hai jo hardware ko nuksaan pahuncha sakti hai, to har shortcut jo
demo ko zyada chamkdaar banata, wo product ko kam bharosemand banata.
